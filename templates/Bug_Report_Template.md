| **Priority** | High / Medium / Low |

## Severity vs Priority

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Blocker | Testing or main business flow cannot continue |
| Critical | Main feature is broken or data is wrong, no workaround |
| Major | Feature does not work correctly but a workaround exists |
| Minor | Small UI or validation issue with low impact |

**Severity** tells how badly the bug affects the application. **Priority** tells how soon it should be fixed.

## Bug Life Cycle

`New > Assigned > Open > Fixed > Retest > Verified > Closed` (or `Reopened` if the fix fails)
