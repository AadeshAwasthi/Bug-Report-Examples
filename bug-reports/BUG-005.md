## Description

Button stays "Remove" and the cart badge still shows 1. This is different from the expected behaviour seen with `standard_user` and from normal e-commerce behaviour.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com
2. Login with `problem_user` / `secret_sauce`
3. Click "Add to cart" on Sauce Labs Backpack
4. Click "Remove" on the same product

## Expected Result

Product is removed, button changes to "Add to cart" and badge is cleared.

## Actual Result

Button stays "Remove" and the cart badge still shows 1.

## Reproducibility

Always (3 out of 3 attempts)

Not attached (behaviour is easy to reproduce with the steps above).
