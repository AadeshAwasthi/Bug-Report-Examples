## Description

Prices on the Products page are different after each refresh and do not match the price shown on the detail page. This is different from the expected behaviour seen with `standard_user` and from normal e-commerce behaviour.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com
2. Login with `visual_user` / `secret_sauce`
3. Note the price of every product
4. Refresh the page
5. Compare the prices again

## Expected Result

Prices stay the same after refresh (for example Backpack $29.99).

## Actual Result

Prices on the Products page are different after each refresh and do not match the price shown on the detail page.

## Reproducibility

Always (3 out of 3 attempts)

Not attached (behaviour is easy to reproduce with the steps above).
