## Description

Products page loads after a delay of about 5 seconds with no loading indicator. This is different from the expected behaviour seen with `standard_user` and from normal e-commerce behaviour.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com
2. Enter `performance_glitch_user` / `secret_sauce`
3. Click Login and measure the time until Products page is visible

## Expected Result

Products page loads within 2 seconds, same as standard_user.

## Actual Result

Products page loads after a delay of about 5 seconds with no loading indicator.

## Reproducibility

Always (3 out of 3 attempts)

Not attached (behaviour is easy to reproduce with the steps above).
