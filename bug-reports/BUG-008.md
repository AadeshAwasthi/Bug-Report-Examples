## Description

Products page loads after a delay of about 5 seconds (5.1 seconds in each of 3 attempts) with no loading indicator. The delay is seen only with `performance_glitch_user`.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com
2. Enter `performance_glitch_user` / `secret_sauce`
3. Click Login and measure the time until Products page is visible

## Expected Result

Products page loads within 2 seconds. With standard_user it loads in less than 1 second.

## Actual Result

Products page loads after a delay of about 5 seconds (5.1 seconds in each of 3 attempts) with no loading indicator.

## Reproducibility

Always (3 out of 3 attempts)

Not attached (behaviour is easy to reproduce with the steps above).
