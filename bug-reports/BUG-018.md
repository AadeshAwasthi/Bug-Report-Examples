## Description

Sort option is reset to "Name (A to Z)". This is different from the expected behaviour seen with `standard_user` and from normal e-commerce behaviour.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com
2. Login with `standard_user` / `secret_sauce`
3. Select "Price (high to low)"
4. Click on any product name
5. Click "Back to products"

## Expected Result

Products are still sorted by Price (high to low).

## Actual Result

Sort option is reset to "Name (A to Z)".

## Reproducibility

Always (3 out of 3 attempts)

Not attached (behaviour is easy to reproduce with the steps above).
