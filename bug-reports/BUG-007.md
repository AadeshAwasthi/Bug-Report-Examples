## Description

A 404 "page not found" page opens. This is different from the expected behaviour seen with `standard_user` and from normal e-commerce behaviour.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com
2. Login with `problem_user` / `secret_sauce`
3. Open the burger menu
4. Click "About"

## Expected Result

Sauce Labs home page opens.

## Actual Result

A 404 "page not found" page opens.

## Reproducibility

Always (3 out of 3 attempts)
