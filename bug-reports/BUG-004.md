## Description

Nothing happens on click. Button stays "Add to cart" and the badge is not updated. The same steps work correctly with `standard_user`.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com
2. Login with `problem_user` / `secret_sauce`
3. Click "Add to cart" on Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt
4. Click "Add to cart" on Sauce Labs Fleece Jacket
5. Observe the cart badge

## Expected Result

Both products are added and the cart badge shows 2.

## Actual Result

Nothing happens on click. Button stays "Add to cart" and the badge is not updated.

## Reproducibility

Always (3 out of 3 attempts)

Not attached (behaviour is easy to reproduce with the steps above).
